**Q5.** Pipe A can fill a tank in 25 minutes; Pipe B can empty the same tank in 20 minutes. If both pipes are opened together, how long until the tank is full?
a) 15 min
b) 18 min
c) 13 min
d) Never fills

---

**Answers:**
1. **b) 6** — A's remaining work after B does 10/15 of it: 1/3 ÷ (1/18) = 6 days
2. **d) None of these** — working it out gives N = 180, which isn't among a/b/c
3. **a) 48 days** — combined rate minus Jake's and Paul's individual rates isolates Hari's
4. **b) 12** — HCF of (148−4), (246−6), (623−11) = HCF(144, 240, 612) = 12
5. **d) Never fills** — Pipe A fills at 4%/min, Pipe B empties at 5%/min; net rate is negative, so the tank drains faster than it fills
