## 🧠 PART 1: THE MASTER FORMULA (LCM METHOD)

This is the **only method** you need for 90% of these problems. It avoids fractions completely.

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| **1** | Take the **LCM** of all given time periods (days/hours/minutes). This = **Total Work** (in units). |
| **2** | Find each person's/pipe's **Efficiency** = `Total Work / Their Time`. |
| **3** | **Add** efficiencies for workers/inlets. **Subtract** efficiencies for emptiers/outlets. |
| **4** | **Required Time** = `Total Work / Net Efficiency`. |

## 👷 PART 2: TIME & WORK (SOLVED EXAMPLES)

### Example 1: Basic Two Workers
> **Q:** A → 10 days, B → 20 days. In how many days can (A+B) complete the work?

| Step | Calculation |
|------|-------------|
| Total Work | LCM(10,20) = **20 units** |
| Efficiencies | A = 20/10 = 2, B = 20/20 = 1 |
| Combined | 2 + 1 = **3 units/day** |
| Time | 20 / 3 = **6⅔ days** |

---

### Example 2: Three Workers
> **Q:** A → 10 days, B → 15 days, C → 20 days. Find time for (A+B+C).

| Step | Calculation |
|------|-------------|
| Total Work | LCM(10,15,20) = **60 units** |
| Efficiencies | A=6, B=4, C=3 |
| Combined | 6+4+3 = 13 units/day |
| Time | **60/13 days** |

---

### Example 3: Four Workers
> **Q:** A=10d, B=20d, C=30d, D=45d. Find time for all together.

| Step | Calculation |
|------|-------------|
| Total Work | LCM(10,20,30,45) = **180 units** |
| Efficiencies | A=18, B=9, C=6, D=4 |
| Combined | 18+9+6+4 = 37 units/day |
| Time | **180/37 days** |

---

### Example 4: Work Left After Few Days
> **Q:** A=30 days, B=60 days. They work together for 5 days. What part of work is left?

| Step | Calculation |
|------|-------------|
| Total Work | LCM(30,60) = 60 units |
| Efficiencies | A=2, B=1 → combined = 3 units/day |
| Work in 5 days | 3 × 5 = 15 units |
| Work left | 60 - 15 = 45 units |
| **Fraction left** | 45/60 = **¾** |

---

### Example 5: Find Individual Time
> **Q:** A alone = 20 days. (A+B) together = 15 days. Find B alone.

| Step | Calculation |
|------|-------------|
| Total Work | LCM(20,15) = 60 units |
| Efficiencies | A = 3, (A+B) = 4 |
| B's efficiency | 4 - 3 = 1 unit/day |
| **B's time** | 60/1 = **60 days** |

---

### Example 6: Complex (With a New Worker C)
> **Q:** A=15d, B=10d. They work together for 4 days, then B leaves. A + C finish remaining in 3 days. How long for C alone to do 40% of work?

| Step | Calculation |
|------|-------------|
| Total Work | LCM(15,10) = 30 units |
| Efficiencies | A=2, B=3 |
| Work in first 4 days | (2+3)×4 = 20 units |
| Remaining work | 30 - 20 = 10 units |
| Efficiency of (A+C) | 10/3 units/day |
| C's efficiency | (10/3) - 2 = 4/3 units/day |
| 40% of total work | 30 × 40% = 12 units |
| **Time for C** | 12 / (4/3) = **9 days** |

## 🚿 PART 3: PIPES & CISTERNS

**Rule:** Inlet = Positive (+), Outlet/Leak = Negative (-).

---

### Example 7: One Inlet + One Outlet
> **Q:** Fill pipe = 8 hrs, Empty pipe = 16 hrs. Time to fill if both open?

| Step | Calculation |
|------|-------------|
| Total Work | LCM(8,16) = 16 units |
| Efficiencies | Inlet = +2, Outlet = -1 |
| Net | 2 - 1 = 1 unit/hr |
| **Time** | 16/1 = **16 hours** |
| **Answer** | (c) 16 |

---

### Example 8: Two Inlets + One Outlet
> **Q:** Two fill pipes = 12 min & 15 min. Waste pipe empties. All together fill in 20 min. How long for waste pipe alone to empty?

| Step | Calculation |
|------|-------------|
| Total Work | LCM(12,15,20) = 60 units |
| Efficiencies | Pipe1 = +5, Pipe2 = +4, All 3 = +3 |
| Waste pipe efficiency | 3 - (5+4) = -6 units/min |
| **Time to empty** | 60/6 = **10 min** |
| **Answer** | (b) 10 |

---

### Example 9: Fill Half Tank
> **Q:** A fills in 12 hrs, B empties in 20 hrs. Time to fill **half** the tank?

| Step | Calculation |
|------|-------------|
| Total Work | LCM(12,20) = 60 units |
| Efficiencies | A = +5, B = -3 → Net = +2 units/hr |
| Half work | 60/2 = 30 units |
| **Time for half** | 30/2 = **15 hours** |

### LCM & HCF of Fractions
| Formula |
|---------|
| LCM of fractions = **LCM(Numerators) / HCF(Denominators)** |
| HCF of fractions = **HCF(Numerators) / LCM(Denominators)** |

---

### Example 10: Basic HCF
> **Q:** Find HCF of 12 and 15.

| Step | Calculation |
|------|-------------|
| Factors | 12 = 1,2,3,4,6,12 &nbsp;&nbsp; 15 = 1,3,5,15 |
| Common | 1, 3 |
| **HCF** | **3** |

---

### Example 11: LCM by Prime Factorization
> **Q:** Find LCM of 24, 32, and 45.

| Step | Calculation |
|------|-------------|
| Prime factors | 24 = 2³×3, 32=2⁵, 45=3²×5 |
| LCM | Take highest powers: 2⁵ × 3² × 5 |
| **LCM** | 32 × 9 × 5 = **1440** |
| **Answer** | (d) 1440 |

---

### Example 12: LCM of Decimals (Trick Question)
> **Q:** Find LCM of 3.6, 1.8, and 0.144.  
> Options: (a) 36 (b) 360 (c) 3.6 (d) 3600

**Trick Method (as per PDF):**
1. Multiply all by 1000 to remove decimals:
   - 3.6 → 3600
   - 1.8 → 1800
   - 0.144 → 144
2. LCM(3600, 1800, 144) = 3600
3. Divide back by 1000 → **3.6**

| **Answer** | (c) 3.6 |

---

### Example 13: Traffic Light Problem (LCM in Real Life)
> **Q:** Lights change every 48s, 72s, 108s. They change together at 8:20 AM. When next together?

| Step | Calculation |
|------|-------------|
| Prime factors | 48=2⁴×3, 72=2³×3², 108=2²×3³ |
| LCM | 2⁴ × 3³ = 16 × 27 = **432 seconds** |
| Convert | 432 sec = 7 min 12 sec |
| Add to 8:20 | **8:27:12 AM** |
| **Answer** | (a) 8:27:12 AM |

## ⚡ PART 5: SHORTCUTS & QUICK TRICKS

### 1. Last Digit of a Product
> **Find last digit of:** 7656 × 7163 × 6353 × 4167

| Step | Calculation |
|------|-------------|
| Multiply only unit digits | 6 × 3 × 3 × 7 |
| Step-by-step | 6×3=18 (unit 8), 8×3=24 (unit 4), 4×7=28 (unit 8) |
| **Last digit** | **8** |

---

### 2. Quick Formula for Two Workers/Pipes
| Situation | Formula |
|-----------|---------|
| A & B together (both filling) | `(A×B) / (A+B)` |
| One fills, one empties | `(A×B) / (B – A)` (where B > A) |

---

### 3. If Someone is "x%" as Efficient
If B is **twice** as efficient as A, then B's time = **half** of A's time.
